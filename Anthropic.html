Anthropic's Example Workspace

The "Get started" article gives a plain example you can copy: task-named folders you point Cowork to one at a time. This is an illustration, not a required layout.

Claude-Workspace/            <- Anthropic's worked example
├── receipts-and-expenses/
├── meeting-notes/
├── research-projects/
│   ├── project-alpha/
│   └── project-beta/
└── reports-and-drafts/

With Projects, each project keeps its own instructions and memory alongside its files:

~/<your workspace>/
└── Projects/
    └── Q2-board-deck/
        ├── project-instructions.md   # auto-read for this project
        ├── files/
        └── memory.md
